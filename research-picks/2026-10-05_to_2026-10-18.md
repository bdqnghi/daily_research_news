# Research picks from my X likes — Oct 5–18, 2026

The research papers, methods and tools from two weeks of likes. Model launches, benchmark results and industry news are left out.

**If you only read five:** Harness², Terminal-RSI, Scaling Discovery Requires Forgetting, Next-Concept Models, VERA.

---

## 1. Recursive self-improvement & auto-research

- **Harness²: Recursive Agent Harnessing for an Open World** (Google Research) — agents improve their own harness for each task they work on; open-sourced
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14224
  https://github.com/google-research/harness2
- **Terminal-RSI** (Tencent Hunyuan) — alternates the agent rewriting its successful trajectories with policy refinement; Qwen3.8-27B goes from 51.7% to 86.5% on terminal tasks
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22192
- **RSI-Anything** — one RSI model, built on world models, for coding, GUI, planning and science tasks
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13912
  https://github.com/Gen-Verse/RSI-Anything
- **Recursive Self-Improvement through Multi-Agent Self-Supervision** (Sakana)
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12176
- **The AI Scientist v3: The Agentic Scientist** (Sakana) — orchestrates coding agents for automated scientific discovery
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18933
  https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist-v3
- **Scaling Discovery Requires Forgetting** (UC Berkeley) — research agents scale better when they forget old ideas
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20563
- **Stateless Language Agents: Scaling Long-Horizon Automated Research** — stops research agents from spending tokens revisiting old ideas
  https://x.com/askalphaxiv/status/2109920072378863832
- **Fork-and-Flush** — treats auto-research as "idea descent" and helps agents escape idea basins
  https://x.com/DimitrisPapail/status/2109661202733212136
- **Do auto-research runs keep exploring?** — no, they settle into a few directions early
  https://x.com/jackcai1206/status/2109101112988950691
- **RSI-top** — helps research agents explore new directions when progress stalls
  https://x.com/Mao_Yuzhen/status/2111165399131652564
- **Two Perspectives on Recursive Self-Improvement** — a survey from existing methods to new research ideas, plus a reading list
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14625
  https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/awesome-rsi
- **Training Reasoning Models to Generate Diverse Scientific Ideas** (Salesforce)
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22324
- **Post-training Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL into an autonomous ML researcher** (Bespoke Labs)
  https://bespokelabs.ai/blog/teaching-nvidias-nemotron-ml-engineering
- **A grader that evolves with the agent** (AWS + HSBC) — no human-written grader needed for self-improvement
  https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2109527405715161230
- **Accept a harness edit only if it beats the current version on fresh tasks**
  https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2110779288698139114

**Benchmarks for self-improving agents**
- **RSI-Inference Bench** (Apple) — https://github.com/apple-aiml-research/ml-rsi-inference-bench
- **GDPevo** — agent self-evolution on real business tasks — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03764
- **ServeLearnBench** — continual self-improvement from serving experience — https://x.com/InfiniAILab/status/2107908386398605313
- **A benchmark of 15 open scientific follow-up problems, with nine frontier agents** — https://x.com/arankomatsuzaki/status/2110737175285502386
