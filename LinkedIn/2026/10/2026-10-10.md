Published: 2026-10-10 13:56 UTC

> **Summary:** Thinking Inertia examines why disabling explicit thinking does not necessarily remove reasoning. DeepSeek-V4-Flash reportedly still verbalizes reasoning in 99.9% of open-ended answers. Direct-answer constraints work better for binary questions than open-ended tasks; across five models, forcing answer-only responses raises compliance to roughly 40% but reduces accuracy by around 15 points. Output tags alone are an unreliable measure of reasoning effort.
