> **Summary:** AAArena evaluates heuristic learning across 12 programming games against 1,920 archived human bots. Coding agents improve their strategies from match replays, sometimes reaching strong leaderboard positions, but complex rule systems remain difficult. Increasing the match budget does not reliably fix stalled agents, distinguishing additional experimentation from actual learning progress.
