> **Summary:** A controlled ARC-AGI-3 study lets models write a harness, test it on five development games, and rewrite it twice. Across 27 lineages, the reported held-out gain is only 2.80 points, with a confidence interval crossing zero. Development improvements exaggerate generalization, and cheaper revisions can reduce performance. The result cautions against assuming autonomous harness revision reliably improves an agent.
