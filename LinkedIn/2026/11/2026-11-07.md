> **Summary:** Re-analyzes MIT CSAIL's 'Sparks of Weight-Space Programming': networks GPT-6 Astra writes for MinAtar games are 95-97% zeros with few distinct values and can be rewritten exactly as short programs (conditional rules, lookup tables). They make poor initializations for PPO, and the model's equivalent Python code beats its own networks. Conclusion: LLMs can write programs in network form, not meaningful trained-like weights.
