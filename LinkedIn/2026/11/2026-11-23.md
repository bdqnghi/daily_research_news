# LinkedIn Likes — 2026-11-23

## Key Topics

- **Haibo Ding** — Haibo Ding announces AWS's Strands Decider v2, a family of five Apache-2.0 open decision models (E2B, E4B, 12B, 26B-A4B, 31B) using prefix-bidirectional attention, which he says beats v1 on accuracy and calibration and adds image input.
- **Charlie Warren** — Charlie Warren is hiring a Director for YC Early Access, a new YC program connecting technology buyers with batch and alumni YC companies.
- **Pushmeet Kohli** — Pushmeet Kohli says Gemini-powered Google DeepMind agents found quantum algorithms that cut estimated compute costs by 10.9x for a fusion simulation, 8.1x for photochemistry and 2.5x for a drug-metabolism molecule, with each circuit result proved in Lean.
- **Sahil kapoor** — Sahil Kapoor describes Google's Agent Substrate, which he says has been donated to the CNCF, as a Kubernetes approach to running mostly idle AI agents without keeping a dedicated pod per agent or paying long cold starts on resume.
- **Ben Burtenshaw** — Ben Burtenshaw (Hugging Face) shares a report and guide on building an agentic eval from a real physics-lab example, covering turning workflows into test cases, writing graders, partial publication and keeping the benchmark useful as models improve.
- **DAIR.AI** — DAIR.AI summarizes a Cornell paper reporting that RL post-training on knowledge tasks makes models memorize hallucinations from their own reasoning chains: recall of rewarded hallucinations rose 4.83% on average versus 0.17% for unrewarded ones, and rewarded hallucinations made models 7.23% less likely to abstain.
- **Elvis Saravia** — Elvis Saravia summarizes Alibaba's CoEvoBench, in which an LLM researcher gets a 9B base model, a starting harness and 24 hours to co-evolve model and harness; he reports GPT-6 Astra closed over four-fifths of the gap to an instruct model and that the harness drove most gains in four of five domains.
- **Bret Taylor** — Bret Taylor announces Sierra's Series F of $1.75B at a $30B valuation, led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed, and says Sierra builds agents with almost 50% of the Fortune 50.
- **Zain Hasan** — Zain Hasan highlights lessons from Anthropic's guide to evaluating AI agents, such as starting with 20 to 50 tasks taken from real failures, grading outcomes rather than exact tool-call sequences, and fixing the eval itself (Opus 4.5 reportedly went from 42% to 95% on CORE-Bench after grading and scaffold fixes).
- **Meghana Aparna Sistla** — Meghana Aparna Sistla (Google DeepMind) presents Tether, a verified stateful policy engine that sandboxes each coding-agent command, infers actions from system calls, tracks sensitivity labels, and checks them against a Lean 4 policy; the authors report it blocks 100% of attacks on modeled trajectories in MonitoringBenchHonest and LinuxArena with a 2.3-5.0% false-positive rate.
