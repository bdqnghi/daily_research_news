### Furu Wei
[Post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/weifuru_llm-as-a-coach-experiential-learning-for-share-7485614975391236096-_LZU/) · [Author](https://www.linkedin.com/in/weifuru/)

Published: 2026-07-22 08:41 UTC

> **Summary:** Experiential Learning turns an LLM evaluator into a coach that converts response-level assessments into reusable textual knowledge. That knowledge conditions a teacher and is internalized by the student through on-policy context distillation. The post argues that this richer feedback preserves distinctions lost in scalar rewards, with reported improvements in generalization and reduced reward hacking on non-verifiable tasks.
