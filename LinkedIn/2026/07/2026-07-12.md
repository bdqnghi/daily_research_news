> **Summary:** “Measuring the Gap Between Human and LLM Research Ideas” compares proposals generated from prior work associated with 11,683 real papers. Rather than only judging individual novelty, the study labels the gaps and contribution types each proposal explores. Paul highlights a diversity gap: model ideas cover a narrower range of research patterns than the corresponding human work.
