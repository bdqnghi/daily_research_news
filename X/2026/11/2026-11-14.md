> 
> We tested 𝟭𝟴 frontier models on 𝟮𝟰 tasks, from 𝗿𝗼𝗯𝗼𝘁 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝘁𝗿𝗼𝗹 and 𝟯𝗗 𝗿𝗲𝗰𝗼𝗻𝘀𝘁𝗿𝘂𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 to 𝗿𝗲𝗮𝘀𝗼𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴 and 𝘃𝗶𝘀𝘂𝗮𝗹 𝗰𝗿𝗲𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻—𝟭𝟴𝟴 runs and nearly 𝟯,𝟴𝟬𝟬 rounds of self-improvement. Agents could revise their code, tools, prompts, and workflows while their model weights stayed fixed.
> 
> A few observations I found particularly interesting:
> 
> 🛠️ 𝗕𝘂𝗶𝗹𝗱𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗿𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗮𝗹𝘀 𝗺𝗼𝗿𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗮𝗻 𝗮𝗻𝘀𝘄𝗲𝗿𝗶𝗻𝗴.
> The largest gains came when agents built programs, robot policies, or pipelines. Models starting from the same solution ended up far apart.
> 
> 📉 𝗙𝗲𝗲𝗱𝗯𝗮𝗰𝗸 𝗰𝗮𝗻 𝗯𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗯𝗼𝘁𝘁𝗹𝗲𝗻𝗲𝗰𝗸.
> Open-weight models made progress with graded feedback but often stalled when they received only success or failure. On RoboCasa, median validation gains were 51.2 points for closed models versus 5.6 for open-weight models.
> 
> 🧱 𝗟𝗼𝗼𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗿𝗶𝗴𝗵𝘁 𝗶𝘀 𝗮 𝗹𝗼𝘄 𝗯𝗮𝗿.
> Some reconstructed scenes looked convincing but failed physical stability or object-level checks. The gap between closed and open-weight models was often largest where correctness could be tested strictly.
