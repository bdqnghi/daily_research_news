### @CrazyShyyt (CrazyShyyt)
[Tweet](https://x.com/CrazyShyyt/status/2121489670190358557)

> China did it again!
> 
> They just cracked Recursive Self-Improvement at the pre-training level.
> 
> Until now, recursive self-improvement was only used after a model was already built.
> 
> frontier pre-training is usually a massive, unrepeatable mess.. you throw trillions of tokens at a cluster and leave behind a vast, chaotic trail of hyperparameter changes, infrastructure diffs, and data edits that no human team can fully track or remember.
> 
> these researchers just fixed that by bringing rsi directly into the pre-training phase.
> 
> instead of one blind run, they broke the massive training process into 6 closed autonomous loops.. the ai agents literally drove the production line:
> 
> - an infra debugging loop and data debugging loop that autonomously resolved cross-stack diagnoses and output anomalies
> 
> - experience-to-metric loops that handled site discovery, web crawling, and generating agentic training data at a scale no human team could manage
> 
> - a recursive engine that recorded what failed and why, persisting that knowledge so the ai learned how to train itself better for the next iteration
> 
> the result?
> 
> a model called 801-30B-A3B (named after the classroom where it was born)..
> 
> the benchmarks are insane. as a raw base model, 801-30B completely outperforms Qwen3-30B and Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B, trailing only the much larger Qwen3.5-35B.
> 
> but it gets crazier.. with just a lightweight supervised fine-tune (only 6B tokens, zero reinforcement learning), it achieved the highest average on four agentic benchmarks against fully post-trained models.
> 
> the gap between human-engineered models and agent-engineered models is closing fast. the next generation of frontier models won't be built by us.. they will be built by this exact autonomous architecture.
> 
> the engine of ai creation became self-improving..
