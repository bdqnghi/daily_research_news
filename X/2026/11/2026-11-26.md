### @rohanpaul_ai (Rohan Paul)
[Tweet](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2126032236487319905)

> Researchers from Scale AI and Carnegie Mellon found that PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization) with a critic beat GRPO ( Group Relative Policy Optimization) at small RL batch sizes, while GRPO caught up as batches grew, across more than 1,200 training runs.
> 
> Since DeepSeek-R1, most open RL work for LLMs dropped PPO's critic, a second model that scores each step, in favor of GRPO. Recent papers say critics win again, but they usually test 1 batch size.
> 
> They ran over 1,200 training runs, 96 with LLMs, comparing both methods on control tasks, math, and multi-turn terminal agents across many batch sizes.
> 
> In terminal agents, PPO led by up to 5.9 points at batch sizes 128 and 512, and all methods tied within 0.6 points at 2,048. PPO at batch 128 matched GRPO at 512 using 91 versus 342 GPU-hours.
