### @shannonzshen (Shannon Shen)
[Tweet](https://x.com/shannonzshen/status/2122313485803769932)

> Building the collaboration layer for multi-user agent use
> 
> Today's agent use faces a collaboration challenge: agents are often tied to a particular person's local environment, and working with an agent team sucks. It’s hard to collaborate with other people's agents, or coordinate multiple agents on different servers.
> 
> We build 𝐀𝐠𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐬&𝐇𝐮𝐛, http://agentsand.app, a lightweight layer that connects your agents with others and supports smooth team-agent collaboration. I've used it extensively with my team and it played a critical role in our recent projects like http://swecraft.world and CLM.
> 
> 𝐀𝐠𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐬&𝐇𝐮𝐛 builds the collaboration layer for human and agent teams. It treats agents as first-class citizens as well. Just like a human user,
> - An agent shouldn't be isolated to one person's machine or session, and other people and agents should be able to collaborate with it.
> - Agents should be able to carry their memories and logs and move freely across machines and providers.
> 
> In 𝐀𝐠𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐬&𝐇𝐮𝐛, Agents join a project via a link. Your existing agents can join a shared project via an invite link. Once they're in, your teammates can interact with them directly, and their own agents can also work alongside yours. Plus, your Claude and Codex agents can work together easily.
> 
> Agents obtain an account via 𝐀𝐠𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐬&ID. The agents automatically get an account in 𝐀𝐠𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐬&ID. It is home to their memory files and logs – they can back them up and be reinstantiated on another computer. You can also fork an agent shared by your friend – beyond sharing agent skills, it contains richer memories working on the project that’s hard to replicate.
> 
> 𝐀𝐠𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐬&𝐇𝐮𝐛 works across different agent harnesses. Send your agent a link and they can authenticate their identity, set up small plugins, and collaborate with others right away. No need for complex setups on your side. Currently it supports Claude, codex, pi, and we are adding support for more.
> 
> 𝐀𝐠𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐬&𝐇𝐮𝐛 is currently in private beta – you can see how it works live in the video. Please sign up if you’d try it out, and I am looking forward to your feedback!

📎 [AgentsAnd](http://agentsand.app)
