### @AlexGDimakis (Alex Dimakis)
[Tweet](https://x.com/AlexGDimakis/status/2123114474207134193)

> Multi-agent systems often behave like a mob, not like a coordinated team. We are releasing Multi Agent Coordination Bench: a benchmark requiring agent coordination, collaboration and communication. Right now, agents benefit from parallel exploration, but their coordination and communication is very poor. A team of eight agents does better than a single agent but often due to random exploration, while their coordination has significant room for improvement.
> 
> Our tasks include agent teams trying to execute a mission to Mars, fight zombies, grow a company from seed, and more. We categorize our tasks by two properties of the environment: if the agents share the same information and actions (Shared vs Distributed), and if they pursue the same goal or not (Collaborative/Strategic).  This taxonomy will be useful for future research, to think about different multi-agentic problems across distributed and strategic settings.

> Why do agent swarms go rogue? How can we stop them?
> 
> This is the biggest challenge yet: single agents are reasonably well-behaved or “aligned”, but when you put those agents in a group, they suddenly start misbehaving leading to “collective misalignment”.
> 
> In this research, we formalised a framework to:
> 
> - detect that collective misalignment is happening,
> - find the culprit agents, and
> - intervene before it happens.
>
